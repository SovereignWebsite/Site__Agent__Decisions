Decisions

Dinis Cruz runs a team of AI agents. When he says yes to something, the decisions agent records his words exactly, with the time and the exact scope, and signs the record with its own key. An agent acts only on a signed record that it has checked.

60 asks waiting for Dinis: the open asks. 37 records so far, 10 in force: see them all.

  2. His signed link there: he operates the decisions agent's key sha256:eefbe996d9c67a7c until 30 November 2026.
  3. The same key in the team's published keys (team/keys.json).
  4. Its signature on each record, and the record's scope against the commits an agent pushes.
